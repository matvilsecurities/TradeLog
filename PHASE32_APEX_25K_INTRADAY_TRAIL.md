# Phase 32 — Apex 25K Intraday Trail / Tradovate Accounts

Added the Apex Trader Funding 25K Intraday Trail Tradovate variants to Prop Firm Setup:

- Standard — $59 activation fee
- No Activation Fee — $0 activation fee

Both variants include the 25K Intraday Trail evaluation rules captured from Apex's current product pages: $1,500 profit target, $1,000 intraday trailing drawdown, 4 mini / 40 micro maximum during evaluation, one-day minimum, 30-day access period, 7-day PA activation deadline, 20 maximum accounts, and 100% payout split. Apex's current pages also show 2 mini / 20 micro for the 25K PA and a 50% consistency rule for the PA.

The standard promo pricing shown by Apex's product page is $16.70 one-pack / $74.95 five-pack at the time of research. The no-activation pricing displayed in the user's supplied screenshot is $14.70 one-pack / $64.95 five-pack and is marked in the UI as a screenshot pricing snapshot that should be verified at checkout.

Vendor/platform is explicitly stored as Tradovate. Prop Firm Setup now exposes a vendor selector when a firm has vendor-specific programs.
